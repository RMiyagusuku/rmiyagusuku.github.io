Our new paper [Stereo Camera Visual SLAM with Motion-state Classification and Mask Refinement in Dynamic Outdoor Environments Containing Large Moving Objects]() has been accepted for publication in the Journal *"Advanced Robotics"*

#### Recommended citation
R. Bao, R. Komatsu, R. Miyagusuku, M. Chino, A. Yamashita, and H. Asama, "Stereo camera visual SLAM with motion-state classification and mask refinement in dynamic outdoor environments containing large moving objects," *Advanced Robotics*, vol. 35, 2021.
