Participated in IROS 2026@Pittsburgh, which featured outstanding research presentations, lively discussions, and company demonstrations showcasing the latest advances in robotics and technology. Together with Kahori Kita (Research Associate, SRALab@Northwestern University) and Sarthak Pathak (Associate Professor, Shibaura Institute of Technology), we organized the [SICE](https://www.sice.jp/) Luncheon, featuring an invited talk by Dr. Katsu Yamane ([Path Robotics](https://www.katsuyamane.com/)) titled *"A Robotics Journey: From Academia to Industry Research to Startup."*; and an introduction of SICE activities by the President of SICE Dr. Yuji Yasui. The event was a great success, attracting more participants than expected. We look forward to continuing this initiative at IROS 2027 in Florence, Italy.
