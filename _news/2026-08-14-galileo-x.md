The work our laboratory is carrying out on robotics and machine learning technologies for agriculture was featured on BS Nittere's program "Galileo X".

The program included interviews with Prof. Koichi Ozaki and Asst. Prof. Renato Miyagusuku, along with students working on smart agriculture research, and introduced the "Agricultural Support Robot" and the "Early Disease and Pest Diagnosis System using Machine Learning" that our laboratory is developing.

* Program: Galileo X 「躍進するフィジカルAI開発 次世代ロボットは何ができるのか?」
* Broadcast: BS Nittere
* Air dates: August 6 (Thu), August 13 (Thu), 2026
* [Program website](https://www.bs4.jp/galileoX/)
